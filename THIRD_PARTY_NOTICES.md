# THIRD PARTY NOTICES

NEUL v0.19 uses a native WebGL2 venue renderer and has no third-party 3D runtime dependency.

## Official venue references
NEUL reconstructs its own geometry from publicly available official venue information, including seat finders, seating maps, floor plans, technical drawings and published venue dimensions. v0.19 specifically refines Taipei Music Center, TICC and Kaohsiung Music Center from their official venue materials.

The original official drawings, logos, photographs and trademarks are not redistributed as NEUL assets unless separately permitted. NEUL's interactive geometry, materials and user interface are independently created.

## External fan-view references
NEUL may link users to twconcertview.com for independently hosted fan-submitted seat-view references. NEUL does not bundle, mirror, hotlink, bulk-copy, or redistribute those fan photographs or that site's proprietary map/interface data.

Publicly visible seat/row observations are used only as manual cross-checks for broad geometric calibration such as:
- rear-row depth,
- front rail / glass risk,
- side-angle behavior,
- overhead equipment,
- aisle / control-booth placement,
- far-view behavior.

Actual event staging and equipment can materially change any sightline. Users should always verify the organizer and ticketing platform's current event notice.

## Typography
NEUL v0.19 optionally loads **Noto Sans KR** and **Noto Sans TC** from Google Fonts for a consistent Korean-editorial UI across platforms. These typefaces are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. If the web fonts are unavailable or the PWA is offline, NEUL falls back to platform fonts such as Apple SD Gothic Neo, PingFang TC, Malgun Gothic and system sans-serif fonts; no font binaries are bundled with this project package.
