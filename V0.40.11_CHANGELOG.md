# NEUL v0.40.11 Changelog

- Added Full Coverage Auditor.
- Added independent official venue discovery for Taipei Music Center, TICC, Zepp New Taipei, and Kaohsiung Music Center / LIVE WAREHOUSE.
- Venue-calendar-only events are automatically backfilled into the event feed and marked for ticket-source follow-up.
- Added coverage-gap and source-health metadata to `/api/events`.
- Added `/api/coverage` health endpoint.
- KKTIX now scans a rotating deep-page window and automatically samples organizer subdomains discovered from official KKTIX event links.
- Added Offtime Music and LDH KKTIX organizer indexes.
- Ticket discovery health now reports parsed counts, errors, and KKTIX promoter roots checked.
- `/api/refresh` now returns a compact coverage summary.
- Future-event automatic custom 3D behavior remains unchanged: every new event receives a unique event layout and later upgrades from official seat-map/price evidence.
- No visual redesign. `index.html` and `styles.css` are byte-identical to v0.40.10.
