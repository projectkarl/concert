# NEUL v0.40.14 — Venue Calibration Merge

Date: 2026-09-23

This release merges the fresher v0.40.13 Verified316 / recent-data branch with the later Taipei Dome recalibration, while preserving the existing NEUL UI.

## Venue / 3D corrections

- Taipei Dome keeps the non-symmetric baseball-bowl footprint and its dedicated broad arched-roof renderer instead of reverting to a generic oval arena.
- Taipei Dome section positioning now honors per-section `centerX` / `centerZ` offsets, improving the asymmetric bowl shape and camera placement.
- NTSU Linkou Arena now stores official upper / middle sub-section metadata and published seat-capacity metadata where verified.
- NTSU lower activity seating is no longer exposed as invented permanent ticket zones. It remains a structural, event-flexible envelope and must be replaced by the event's official configuration when available.
- Taipei Music Center 1F is treated as event-flexible rather than permanent fixed seating.
- Taipei Music Center 2F official section capacities map to 1,463 seats.
- Taipei Music Center 3F keeps the official published total of 1,675 separately from the visible section-label sum of 1,671; NEUL records the 4-seat delta rather than inventing a section assignment.
- Taipei Music Center current official PDF remains the geometry / capacity source for these metadata fields.

## Public UI / source hygiene

- Internal sightline-photo calibration sources are no longer exposed as a public source row or public “實拍視角” link.
- Public source presentation remains focused on official ticket / promoter / venue information.

## Data branch retained

- Keeps v0.40.13 Verified316 discovery / reconciliation behavior.
- Keeps newest-first entertainment news with the recent-only retention window and progressive “看更多”.
- Keeps healthy upcoming-snapshot recovery for thin crawls.
- Keeps Archive capped to the 20 most recent completed events.

## Conservative calibration policy

- Fixed venue structure and event-specific layouts are kept separate. OCR / Vision may alter event-flexible floor or activity-seat areas but may not silently rewrite fixed venue tiers.
- Taipei Arena per-zone row-depth is intentionally not blanket-increased in this release. It still needs row-by-row official calibration; NEUL avoids filling uncertain row maxima with guesses.
- Taipei Music Center 2F remains at 15 rows in the base metadata after re-checking the current official drawing; v0.40.14 does not invent a 16th row.
