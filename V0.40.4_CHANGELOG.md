## Automation
- OCR mapping now uses event-specific effective sections.
- OCR attempts Traditional Chinese + English, with an English fallback.
- Full-width text and section ranges such as VIP A～E / 特A～C / 黃3A～J are normalized and expanded before price matching.
- The OCR `STAGE` token can anchor dark-component selection so legends/background blocks are less likely to become the stage.
- Stage topology detection requires real diagonal evidence before generating an X stage.
- High-confidence gate for overwriting calibrated special-stage geometry.
- OCR-adjacent swatch detection replaces unsafe palette-order price guessing.
- Secondary/ticket URLs are eligible for automatic seat-map resolution.
- Ticket Plus discovery cap increased to 64.
